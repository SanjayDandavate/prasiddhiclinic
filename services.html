Services

Consultations offered at the clinic. To discuss whether a service is right for you, call or WhatsApp the clinic.

  • Gynaecology consultations

    General gynaecological check-ups and concerns.

  • Pregnancy care

    Regular check-ups during pregnancy and after delivery.

  • Menstrual health

    Irregular, painful or heavy periods, and PCOS.

  • Infertility consultation

    Consultation for couples who are having difficulty conceiving.

  • Family planning and contraception

    Contraception advice and pre-pregnancy counselling.

  • Menopause care

    Guidance for symptoms around menopause.

  • Preventive check-ups

    Pap smear and routine screening.
