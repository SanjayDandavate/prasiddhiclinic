How to book

Appointments are arranged directly with the clinic. Walk-ins are also welcome.

  1. Contact the clinic

    Call or WhatsApp during consultation hours. The clinic receptionist will help you.

  2. Agree a time

    The receptionist confirms a day and time that suits you.

  3. Visit the clinic

    Bring any previous reports, prescriptions and scans with you.

Ready to book a consultation?

Call or WhatsApp the clinic during consultation hours. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead is preferred.
